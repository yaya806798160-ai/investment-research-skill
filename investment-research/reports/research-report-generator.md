# Research Report Generator

## Objective
定义自动投资研究报告生成流程：输入研究标的，调用多分析师，输出标准化投资研究报告。

## 输入
- Stock / Fund / Theme：研究标的（股票/基金/行业主题）
- Market：市场（A股/港股/美股等）
- Investment Horizon：投资期限

## 调用流程
1. Industry Analyst：行业地图、周期、竞争格局。
2. Company Analyst：商业模式、成长、护城河、财务质量。
3. Valuation Analyst：估值与隐含预期。
4. Risk Manager：风险、回撤、仓位建议。

## 输出标准

# Investment Research Report

## Executive Summary
- Investment rating
- Main thesis
- Key risks

## Industry Analysis
- Industry position
- Cycle
- Competition

## Company Analysis
- Business model
- Growth
- Moat
- Financial quality

## Valuation
- Current valuation
- Historical comparison
- Implied expectations

## Scenario Analysis
- Bear case
- Base case
- Bull case

## Decision
- Buy
- Add
- Hold
- Wait
- Reduce
- Exit
