# TAM-Adjusted PEG

## Objective
Test whether a high-growth valuation is supported by realistic market opportunity.

## Process
1. Estimate realistic TAM.
2. Estimate serviceable market.
3. Estimate plausible long-term market share.
4. Translate share into revenue.
5. Estimate normalized margin.
6. Estimate sustainable EPS/FCF growth.
7. Compare valuation with sustainable growth.

## Rule
Do not justify a valuation using a TAM that the company cannot realistically monetize.

Use multiple scenarios for share capture rather than a single point estimate.
