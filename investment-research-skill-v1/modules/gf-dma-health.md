# Growth-Fundamental-DMA Health

## Objective
Combine fundamental quality with technical health without allowing technical signals to replace fundamental analysis.

## Fundamental checks
- growth
- margins
- cash generation
- balance sheet
- competitive position
- earnings revisions

## DMA checks
- 20D
- 50D
- 100D
- 200D

Assess alignment, slope, price distance, volume, support/resistance, and drawdown.

## Interpretation
Fundamentals determine whether the asset deserves capital.
Technical health helps determine when and how aggressively to deploy it.
