# Juglar Cycle

## Objective
Analyze medium-term investment/capex cycles.

## Inputs
- capacity
- utilization
- lead times
- inventory
- pricing
- capex
- customer capex
- supply additions
- demand growth

## Cycle map
Classify:
1. Recovery
2. Early expansion
3. Mid expansion
4. Late expansion
5. Slowdown
6. Contraction

## Interpretation
In cyclical industries, high current margins can be a warning rather than proof of durable earnings.

Compare current valuation with the likely normalized earnings level, not only peak earnings.
