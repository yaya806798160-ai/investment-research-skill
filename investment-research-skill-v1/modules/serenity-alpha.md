# Serenity Alpha

## Objective
Prevent narrative-driven investing by separating business quality from price attractiveness.

## Framework
Score independently:
- Quality
- Growth
- Durability
- Expectations
- Valuation
- Timing
- Risk

A strong score in quality does not compensate automatically for extreme valuation.

## Anti-FOMO rules
Flag:
- vertical price moves without fundamental revisions
- valuation expansion ahead of earnings
- consensus crowdedness
- thematic excitement unsupported by economics
- buying solely because a security has already risen

## Required question
"What must go right for today's price to work?"

Then identify what could cause those assumptions to fail.
