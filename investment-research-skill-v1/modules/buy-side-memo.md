# Buy-Side Memo

## Required structure

### 1. Verdict
State:
- action
- conviction
- time horizon
- principal reason

### 2. Thesis
Maximum three major thesis points.

### 3. Evidence
Separate facts from interpretation.

### 4. Industry
Explain cycle, supply/demand, competitive structure, and bottlenecks.

### 5. Company
Explain quality, growth, moat, margins, cash flow, and execution.

### 6. TAM
Show realistic market opportunity and share capture.

### 7. Valuation
Show current valuation and reverse-engineered expectations.

### 8. Scenarios
Bear / base / bull with probabilities and values.

### 9. Technical
Trend, DMA structure, support/resistance, and entry conditions.

### 10. Risks
Rank risks by probability and impact.

### 11. Invalidation
State exactly what evidence would make the thesis wrong.

### 12. Action
Buy / add / hold / wait / reduce / exit / watchlist.

### 13. Monitoring
List the few indicators that matter most.
